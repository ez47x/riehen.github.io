The niche side wins

North 4125 has genuine identity — a place you explore, not pass through. The vineyards, border stones, and forest make it rare for a municipality this close to a major city. South 4125 is pleasant, but dissolves quietly into Basel. Riehen is best when it stays small, rural, and slightly forgotten.
