The two sides

Same postcode,
different worlds

Both carry the 4125 postcode, but north and south Riehen have entirely different characters. One feels like a village that forgot to become a suburb. The other didn't.

North Riehen

Vineyards,
forest &
border land

Rolling vineyards crossing into Germany, the Herrenwald forest, the quirky Eiserne Hand border protrusion, and hiking trails that straddle two countries. Rural, quiet, and genuinely its own place.

South Riehen

River walk,
tram stops
& suburbs

The Wiese River promenade, leafy residential streets, and quick tram access into Basel. Pleasant and livable, but more of a gateway to the city than a destination in itself.

The verdict

So which side wins?

Stripping away shared parks and border zones — looking only at 4125 itself.

Winner: North

The niche side wins

North 4125 has genuine identity — a place you explore, not pass through. The vineyards, border stones, St. Chrischona, and the Herrenwald make it rare for a municipality this close to a major city. South 4125 is pleasant, but quietly dissolves into Basel. Riehen is best when it stays small, rural, and slightly forgotten — and that's the north.
